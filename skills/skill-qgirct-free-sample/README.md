# qgirct free sample — overdue invoice reminder email worked example

A free sample showing what the 4-stage overdue-invoice reminder emails look like, with a worked calculation example. It is a lead-in to the paid tool qgirct (USD 3) at https://sinhscribe.gumroad.com/l/qgirct .

## What is inside

- `SKILL.md` — the free sample: the four stages (1-7 / 8-21 / 22-44 / 45+ days), what each email asks for, and a worked example (USD 1,000 invoice, 18% annual rate: 15 days late = stage 2, original amount only; 30 days late = stage 3, original amount plus USD 14.79 late interest).
- `LICENSE` — MIT license for this free sample package.

## How to use it

1. Open `SKILL.md`.
2. Read the four-stage table and the worked example.
3. If you want the actual tool that prints the reminder email and calculation for your own invoices, get the full tool qgirct at https://sinhscribe.gumroad.com/l/qgirct (USD 3).

## Limits

- This sample does not send any email, read your inbox, or save any personal data. It is a document you read.
- The numbers are worked examples the team verified by running their own script; they are not legal or tax advice.
- Late interest uses actual days / 365 at the annual rate you enter.
